FORMULA

How is it calculated?

WATER = ROOF AREA × RAINFALL × RUNOFF COEFFICIENT × COLLECTION EFFICIENCY

Annual harvestable water (L) = roof area (m²) × annual rainfall (mm/year) × roof runoff coefficient × collection efficiency. The displayed values use district planning estimates and do not represent live weather. Since 1 mm of rainfall over 1 m² is approximately 1 litre, the result is shown as an estimated annual collection. Actual yield depends on rainfall timing, first-flush losses, storage overflow, and system condition.
