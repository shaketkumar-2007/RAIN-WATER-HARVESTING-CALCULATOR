03 / FORMULA

How is it calculated?

WATER = ROOF AREA × RAINFALL × RUNOFF COEFFICIENT × COLLECTION EFFICIENCY

The estimate uses annual rainfall, the roof catchment area, the selected roof material, and collection efficiency. Since 1 mm of rainfall over 1 m² is approximately 1 litre, the result is shown as an estimated annual collection.
